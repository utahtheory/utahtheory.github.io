University of Utah Theory Reading Group

We are a student-led reading group in the Kahlert School of Computing, and every week we discuss research from across the field of theoretical computer science. We also use this time slot to host our Theory Seminars (highlighted in gray).

Logistics

Time: Fridays, 12:00-1:00pm.

Location: MEB 3515, or Meldrum Conference Room (WEB 2760) for our Theory Seminars.

Contact: Eli Friedman (eli.friedman at utah.edu). Send questions, comments, or requests to speak.
